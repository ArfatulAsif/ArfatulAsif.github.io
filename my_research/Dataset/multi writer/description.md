Dataset Description
The dataset is a purpose-built, supervised multi-writer corpus of Bangla handwriting comprising 301 pages collected from multiple writers.

Content & Formatting: Contributors were assigned structured writing tasks using continuous plain prose covering diverse academic and general domains, ensuring rich textual and sequential characteristics.


Annotations: Every page is meticulously annotated with ground-truth metadata, capturing writer identities, document groupings, and exact sequential page orders (within-document indexing).


Integrity: The collection protocol was designed to avoid accidental visual shortcuts, providing natural unconstrained handwriting variations across multiple individuals.


Use Cases
This multi-writer sequential dataset is exceptionally well-suited for:

Multi-Writer Document Segmentation & Restructuring: Evaluating algorithms that process unordered pools of pages to simultaneously cluster them by writer style and group them into coherent source documents.


Sequence Reconstruction (Page Ordering): Training and testing discourse-aware models (such as Transformer pointer networks or cross-encoders) to reorder shuffled pages based on semantic continuity.


Open-Set Writer Identification & Verification: Developing and benchmarking biometric models and deep metric learning frameworks (e.g., ArcFace-based classifiers) on regional or Indic script handwriting.


Handwriting Text Recognition (HTR) & Document Analysis: Benchmarking offline handwriting recognition pipelines and semantic embedding generators tailored for Bangla document archives.


Availability
The data analyzed during this study are available from the me upon reasonable request. (Note: Access is subject to the privacy and consent terms under which the handwritten samples were collected, and anonymized derivatives can be provided where full public release is restricted).
